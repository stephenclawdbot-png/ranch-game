# Horse-racing and horse-breeding games: core loops, competitions and retention (comparables for Post Time)

Research date: 2026-10-29. Facts are marked with their date where they are older or time-sensitive. Several sources are guide sites (Deltia's Gaming, Game8, Prydwen, dbltap), fan wikis (umamusu.wiki) and trade press (PocketGamer.biz, Siliconera, Decrypt, Blockchain Gamer, Naavik). Revenue figures are third-party app-store estimates (AppMagic / Sensor Tower), mobile only, not company disclosures.

## Q1. Umamusume: Pretty Derby: training loop, Team Trials, Champions Meeting, Legend/Daily Races, inheritance, scale and community

### Takeaway
Umamusume layers a ~20-minute single-player "career" (a raise-one-horse run) under a daily auto-resolved ladder (Team Trials, stamina-gated, weekly class promotion) and a monthly, week-long, three-round bracketed PvP tournament (Champions Meeting) with capped daily attempts, a winners-only scoring rule, a one-race final, and two leagues split by power (Graded vs Open). The deep "breeding-like" layer is inheritance: finished horses become parents/grandparents whose randomly rolled "sparks" seed future runs, which drives repeated careers (the grind). It earned about $2.8bn lifetime on mobile, but the global (non-JP) version peaked in month 2 and halved by month 3.

### Cited Findings

**Career (training) loop**
- A career runs three in-game years at two turns per month = 72 turns, plus a 6-turn finale period after year three (~78 turns total) — [umamusu.wiki: Career Mode](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Career_Mode) (via search summary); Prydwen counts ~70 turns with ~10 reserved for goal races and ~8 for summer camp — [Prydwen URA Training Guide](https://www.prydwen.gg/umamusume/guides/ura-training-guide).
- One guide says a career takes "around 20 or so minutes if you skip all the dialogue"; daily tasks total 30 minutes to 1 hour by one estimate, while the same guide also lists "Total Dailies Time: 2-3 hours" (internal contradiction; likely casual vs full-optimization) — [Deltia's Gaming: All Daily Missions](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=279816) / [Deltia's: Free Dailies](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=281318) (via search summary).
- Finished careers produce "Veteran" Umamusume, which are then used both as PvP entrants and as legacies (parents) for future careers — [umamusu.wiki: Inheritance](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Inheritance).

**Inheritance / "sparks" (the breeding analogue)**
- Before a career the player picks 2 Veteran legacies ("parents"); each carries its own 2 sub-legacies ("grandparents"), so up to 6 Veterans contribute sparks — [umamusu.wiki: Inheritance](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Inheritance).
- Inheritance ("Inspiration") triggers 3 times per career: at career start, at the Classic-year event and the Senior-year event (both early April) — [umamusu.wiki: Inheritance](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Inheritance).
- Spark types: Blue = base stat (+5 / +12 / +21 initial gain at 1/2/3 stars, star odds tied to the Veteran's stat value); Pink = track/distance/strategy aptitude (+1 grade for 1-3 total stars up to +4 grades for 10-18 stars, capped at A initially); Green = unique-skill hint (guaranteed for 3-star+ Veterans, never for 2-star or lower); White = race-win and skill sparks (G1 wins, scenario sparks such as URA Finale) — [umamusu.wiki: Inheritance](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Inheritance).
- A trainee "cannot be their own Legacy, but can be their own Sub-Legacy"; "Affinity" (compatibility) is influenced by the chosen legacies and the grandparents — [Game8 inheritance guide](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536822) / [Deltia's: Affinity](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=278319) (via search summary).
- The more often a legacy won the same G1 race, the larger the bonus at inheritance — [GameWith](https://gamewith.net/uma-musume/69695) (via search summary).
- Community complaint: the global game has "two glass ceilings... one is the deck, and the other is inheritances" (i.e., gacha support cards and rolled sparks) — [coin-return forum thread](https://forums.coin-return.org/threads/umamusume-pretty-derby-cinema-horses-and-oshi-stuff.1441/page-19) (single forum voice).
- Community tooling: sites like GameTora host legacy lookups and an inheritance guide, and guides explain following top players to borrow their legacies — [GameTora inheritance guide](https://gametora.com/umamusume/inheritance-guide); [Deltia's: Follow Top Players for Legacies](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=284431).

**Team Trials (daily/weekly ladder)**
- Each Team Trials match costs 1 RP; RP max is 5 bars, one bar refills every 2 hours (so ~12/day if checked), refillable with Carrot Jelly; matches auto-resolve quickly and guides recommend spending all RP daily — [Deltia's: Team Trials Explained](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=281576); [Deltia's: How RP works](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=280536) (via search summary).
- Classes 1-6: you are promoted the following week if your score beats a promotion line that "shifts higher as the week progresses"; no promotion past Class 6, staying above the line retains it — [Deltia's: Class Levels](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=286982); [Goonhammer: Understanding Team Trials](https://www.goonhammer.com/understanding-team-trials-in-umamusume-pretty-derby) (via search summary).
- Team size scales with class: Class 1 one uma per race type, Class 2 two, Class 3+ three, total 15 umamusume on the team (5 race types x 3) — [Prydwen Team Trials Guide](https://www.prydwen.gg/umamusume/guides/team-trials-guide) (via search summary).
- Weekly reset timing differs by guide (Monday 3:00 PM UTC vs "every 6 days"); example reward: 250 Carats to retain Class 6; one guide cited ~420,000 points to reach Class 6 and ~450,000 to retain (likely stale, threshold inflates) — [Deltia's: Class 6](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=314416); [gurugamer](https://gurugamer.com/mobile-games/how-to-reach-class-6-in-umamusume-team-trials-25354/amp) (via search summary).

**Champions Meeting (monthly PvP tournament)**
- JP launch 14 May 2021; events named after zodiac cups, each running about one week, roughly monthly; each cup publishes a fixed track/distance/season/going/weather in advance (e.g., Taurus Cup: Tokyo Turf 2400m, Firm, Spring, Sunny) — [umamusu.wiki: Champions Meeting](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Champions_Meeting).
- Later JP events moved to a "Modern Format" named by distance category (MILE 1, DIRT 1, CLASSIC 1, LONG 1, SPRINT 1 ... MILE 6) — [umamusu.wiki: Champions Meeting](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Champions_Meeting).
- Registration opens 3 days before the event; league choice is mandatory and locks at start — [dbltap](https://www.dbltap.com/guides/umamusume-champions-meeting-pvp-mode-explained-schedule); [umamusu.wiki](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Champions_Meeting).
- Leagues: Graded League (requires higher-rank Umamusume; tougher; better rewards) vs Open League (rank-capped). Global Open League cap raised from B to A+ effective 31 May 2026 (Gemini Cup), together with more Monies and Support Points per win, easier titles, and title upgrades on reruns (e.g., "Taurus Cup Platinum ★") — [Siliconera, 2026](https://www.siliconera.com/umamusume-champions-meetings-changes-coming-in-may/); [dbltap](https://www.dbltap.com/guides/umamusume-champions-meeting-pvp-mode-explained-schedule).
- Team: 3 Veteran Umamusume; each race has 9 runners = your 3 + 3 each from 2 other players — [dbltap](https://www.dbltap.com/guides/umamusume-champions-meeting-pvp-mode-explained-schedule).
- Format: Round 1 and Round 2 each last 2 days, up to 4 attempts per day, each attempt = 5 races (max 20 races/day); 1-day preparation period; Round 3 (final) is a single race — [umamusu.wiki](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Champions_Meeting); [dbltap](https://www.dbltap.com/guides/umamusume-champions-meeting-pvp-mode-explained-schedule).
- Scoring is winner-takes-all: only a 1st place by one of your three horses counts as a win — [dbltap](https://www.dbltap.com/guides/umamusume-champions-meeting-pvp-mode-explained-schedule).
- Advancement per umamusu.wiki: Round 1, 3+ wins in an attempt -> Round 2 Group A; 1-2 wins -> Group B. Round 2: Group A with 3+ wins -> Final A; Group A with 1-2 wins or Group B with >=1 win -> Final B; no wins -> eliminated. Final reward depends on group (A/B) and placing — [umamusu.wiki](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Champions_Meeting). dbltap gives slightly different thresholds ("more than three" / "fewer than three") — [dbltap](https://www.dbltap.com/guides/umamusume-champions-meeting-pvp-mode-explained-schedule). Sources conflict on exact cutoffs.
- First global Champions Meeting (Taurus Cup): umamusu.wiki lists GL 22-28 Aug 2025 — [umamusu.wiki](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Champions_Meeting); another source cited 17-27 Aug 2025 (search-summary attribution, unverified). Global Aquarius Cup 1 ran 13-19 Feb 2026 — [umamusu.wiki](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Champions_Meeting).
- Press reception (Siliconera, 28 Aug 2025): "fantastic, infuriating PvP". Known-in-advance conditions let players plan builds (3 stamina-focused contenders, or one ace plus debuffers); "everyone's turned to Gold Ship"; near-wins "stolen away" were the most painful part; the writer had to retrain despite heavy investment but still wanted to build new teams for the next cup — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/umamusume-champions-meeting-is-fantastic-infuriating-pvp/).
- Mirrored r/gachagaming post describes a "brutal, RNG-heavy grind" and says Cygames "really wants you to grind PvP" and monetizes frustration via QoL subscriptions — [r/gachagaming mirror snapshot, May 2026](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1t4gs67/snapshots/2026-05-06T00%3A35%3A55.549125Z) (secondary mirror, single post).

**Legend Races / Daily Races**
- Daily Races unlock at team rank E and pay Support Points, Monies and Carats — [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537620) (via search summary).
- Daily Legend Races are permanent, give at most 1 Star Piece of that character per day, and only on a 1st-place win — [Deltia's: Legend Race](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=279218) (via search summary).

**Scale, revenue, retention**
- Lifetime global mobile revenue ~$2.8bn in slightly over five years; overseas (non-JP) ~5% of lifetime (AppMagic, reported 29 Jun 2026) — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/umamusume-pretty-derby-races-to-100m-on-mobile-in-one-year-overseas/).
- Global launch 26 Jun 2025; overseas first-year mobile gross $105.7m; monthly: M1 $20.2m, M2 $22.5m (peak), M3 $10.4m; daily spend peaked ~$2.2m on 16 Jul 2025 (story update + Kitasan Black support card banner) and took ~3 weeks to return to baseline; has not reached $500k/day on any day in 2026; the North American film release (27 Feb 2026) produced no clear spending surge. US = $74.3m (~70% of overseas) — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/umamusume-pretty-derby-races-to-100m-on-mobile-in-one-year-overseas/).
- First 30 days (JP + overseas) ~$45.4m, split roughly evenly; ~$22m overseas mobile in first month — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/uma-musume-pretty-derby-makes-503-revenue-outside-japan-in-first-global-month); [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/news/76527-umamusume-pretty-derby-gallops-to-us-22-million-in-first-month-of-global-mobile-release). - Steam concurrency (conflicting): one SteamDB-citing report gives 25,505 CCU on release day and a 47,264 peak by 8 Jul 2025; another report claims "over 80k" — reported in coverage surfaced alongside [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/umamusume-pretty-derby-highly-popular-among-core-gamers-with-over-100-games-on-steam-research-says/) and [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/news/76527-umamusume-pretty-derby-gallops-to-us-22-million-in-first-month-of-global-mobile-release) (exact attribution unverified). Sensor Tower research says its Steam players skew "core" (100+ games owned) — [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/umamusume-pretty-derby-highly-popular-among-core-gamers-with-over-100-games-on-steam-research-says/).
- April 2021 (JP only): ranked 3rd top-grossing mobile game worldwide — [Anime News Network](https://admin.animenewsnetwork.com/interest/2021-05-12/uma-musume-ranks-3rd-in-top-grossing-mobile-games-worldwide-for-april-2021/.172693).

### Inferences
- Umamusume's structure maps closely onto Post Time: Team Trials ~ daily entry budget into a weekly promote/retain ladder; Champions Meeting ~ periodic qualifier rounds + a single-race final; Graded/Open ~ league tiers. Its key design choices worth copying: (a) conditions announced days ahead so players "build for the cup"; (b) a capped number of attempts per day (4 x 5 races) across multiple days, so a bad day is recoverable; (c) a two-tier final (A and B finals), so mid-skill players still race a final; (d) a separate rank-capped league so new players have a fair bracket.
- Its pain points (single-meta convergence on one character, RNG near-misses, retraining whole teams each month, monetized QoL) are the risks for Post Time's Season Final. Varying conditions per event and limiting how much one bloodline/"build" dominates are the main levers.
- Inheritance is "breeding as a gacha on outcomes": random spark rolls create the reason to run many careers. Post Time's Mendelian genetics can provide the same "one more foal" pull, with more transparency.
- Revenue halving in month 3 overseas suggests that even the best-in-genre core loop decays quickly without content beats; the biggest spike came from a story + banner update, not media (film).

### Gaps
- Exact Champions Meeting reward tables, and the precise current advancement thresholds (sources conflict).
- Official player counts (DAU/MAU) for JP or global; Cygames does not publish them. Steam CCU attribution is not verified.
- Average daily play time from a reliable survey (only guide estimates found).

## Q2. Derby Stallion (Switch 2020/21, Derby Stallion Masters mobile, Derby Stallion 2) and Winning Post series: breeding depth, nicks/inbreeding, online competitions, reception

### Takeaway
The Japanese owner-breeder sims are the depth benchmark for pedigree theory: Winning Post 10 2026 lists dozens of named mating theories (inbreeding variants, single/double/triple/"force" nicks, line-breeding "burst" types, outcrosses, dosage, Rasmussen factor, vitality complements, novelty crosses) that all feed a hidden "explosive power" potential with health/temperament risks. Derby Stallion's online play is asynchronous: players register horses into "Breeders' Cup" tournaments; its mobile spin-off (Dabimas) still runs (2026) with an annual national championship qualified via points over 3 races. Hard reception/criticism data in English is thin.

### Cited Findings

**Derby Stallion (Switch)**
- Released in Japan 3 Dec 2020 (often cited as 2021) for Switch, 7,800 yen ex-tax; the player starts with a small ranch, 20 million yen and one broodmare, races horses and reinvests winnings to breed stronger horses — [BCN Retail](https://www.bcnretail.com/topics_detail8/20201204_202604.html); [gamer.ne.jp](https://www.gamer.ne.jp/news/202012030013/).
- Famitsu reviewers: beginner-friendly while "breeding theory (配合理論) is intact" so series fans feel at home; one reviewer said presentation is rich but "simple" in a way that may divide players, and noted UI mis-operation (starting a race before preparing) — [Famitsu reviews](https://www.famitsu.com/game/title/34391/reviews) (via search summary).
- Breeders' Cup (BC) return was welcomed; BC lets players stage original races with horses registered by players nationwide — [Famitsu reviews](https://www.famitsu.com/game/title/34391/reviews) (via search summary).
- Update 1.1.0 (17 Dec 2021): Breeders' Cup entry limit raised from 5 to 20 horses; BC race conditions (previously fixed sunny/good turf) now vary; added "VS Legend" mode vs 51 legendary horses (Deep Impact, Tokai Teio, Symboli Rudolf); race balance and breeding theory adjusted; sire data adjusted based on their offspring's results — [4Gamer](https://www.4gamer.net/games/525/G052561/20211210108/).
- Series history: Derby Stallion '98 shipped "new data" with more stallions and mares and pedigree comments that flag inbreeding and nicks, plus BC-registered stallions you could breed to — [Nintendo.co.jp (Derby Stallion 98 column)](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/n02/shvc/bdbj/3.html).
- Derby Stallion 2 (Switch 2) released in Japan September 2026 (announced June 2026); same premise of starting from a single broodmare, planning matings and building your own bloodline — [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/derby-stallion-2-announced-for-switch-2); [Denfaminicogamer](https://news.denfaminicogamer.jp/news/260924g).

**Derby Stallion Masters ("Dabimas", Drecom, mobile)**
- Android launch 1 Nov 2016 — [4Gamer](https://www.4gamer.net/games/352/G035272/20161101143/).
- Still operating in 2026: the 9th "Dabimas Championship" (王座決定戦) opened qualifier entries 8 Jan 2026; qualifiers are scored as total points by placing across 3 races; final scheduled 20 Mar 2026 — [gamebiz](https://gamebiz.jp/news/418813). The first offline national championship was held in 2017 with MLB pitcher Masahiro Tanaka as PR ambassador — [gamer.ne.jp](https://www.gamer.ne.jp/news/201703140013/).

**Winning Post (Koei Tecmo)**
- Owner-breeder sim using real pedigree data; famous real horses (Deep Impact, Almond Eye) obtainable to race or breed; era start points 1973/1984/2005; WP10 introduced horse personalities; WP10 2024 added "Idol Uma Map" and a 50-year "Keiba History" mode; the protagonist can marry and their children can become jockeys for your horses — [Koei Tecmo WP10 beginner page](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/winningpost10/beginner.html). WP10 2024 released 28 Mar 2024 — [Dengeki Online](https://dengekionline.com/article/202605/76420) (via search summary).
- WP10 2026 official manual "配合理論" (mating theory) page lists dozens of theories feeding "explosive power" (爆発力) and sub-stats, including — [WP10 2026 manual](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/manual/WP102026/jp/7300.html):
  - Inbreeding (same ancestor within 4 generations on both sides): explosive power small-to-large and the ancestor's traits intensify; risk to constitution and temperament, reduced if parents are healthy. Variants: "miracle blood" 3x4, full-sibling inbreeding, female-line inbreeding (lower risk).
  - Outbreeding (no shared ancestor within 4 gens): small boosts to explosive power, spirit, intelligence, health, flexibility and racing lifespan.
  - Nicks (sire x broodmare-sire sub-line compatibility, listed in an in-game "Nick File"): single (small), double (medium), triple (large), "force" nick through 4th gen (very large).
  - Line-breeding "burst" types (3+ of the 3rd-generation sires share a sire line with different sub-lines): large-to-very-large.
  - "Blood Activation Cross" (6+ different sire lines among 3rd-gen ancestors) and its combination with inbreeding to reduce the downside if thinner than 3x4.
  - Dosage theory (points 16/8/4/2 for established sire lines across 4 generations, threshold 32) when the dam is in Europe; Rasmussen factor when the dam is in the US.
  - Recessive heredity (grandsire factor skipping a generation); novelty crosses like "Comedy Cross" (cheap sire x expensive dam), "Farewell Cross" (sire aged 24+), "Rival Cross" (parents were rivals), "Look-Alike Cross" (same coat and markings), Lightning/Gale crosses keyed to coat colour.
- WP10 (2023) "world bloodline" status: once a stallion's line reaches it, its factor effects in breeding theory strengthen — [4Gamer](https://www.4gamer.net/games/670/G067034/20230224060/) (via search summary); WP10 2026 adds "Founder lineage" as the highest bloodline honour — [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/games/winning-post-10-2026).
- Strategy guidance in these games: check the mating screen's "danger" (危険度) indicator; high danger makes low-stat or bad-temperament foals more likely — [gameranbu WP9 2021](https://gameranbu.jp/winningpost9/911f96e8b9ab3ff0c3c2) (via search summary).

### Inferences
- These games prove a niche of players will happily master a large catalogue of named pedigree rules, as long as the game surfaces them (Nick File, mating-evaluation screen with danger icon, pedigree comments). Post Time's Mendelian genetics could add a visible "mating report" naming effects (inbreeding coefficient, line-cross bonuses) to get the same theorycrafting pull.
- Making founding sires/lines "established" over time (world bloodline / founder lineage) is a long-horizon goal that rewards multi-season breeding programs — a fit for Post Time's 4-week seasons.
- Asynchronous "register your horse into a tournament" (Breeders' Cup, Dabimas championship with 3 points races then a final) is a proven, low-infrastructure online model for breeding sims.

### Gaps
- No English-language reviews or forum data on players' specific complaints about Derby Stallion's online/BC or Derby Stallion's own nick/inbreeding rules; Derby Stallion's precise breeding formulas are not documented in sources found (the detailed list above is Winning Post's).
- Sales figures for Derby Stallion Switch / Derby Stallion 2, and Drecom revenue for Dabimas, not found.
- Whether Derby Stallion 2's online mode changes the BC format.

## Q3. Zed Run (2019-2025) and Photo Finish LIVE: cadence, paid-entry races, breeding, price bubble/crash, players, decline, lessons

### Takeaway
Zed Run ran 12-horse races "several per minute", with paid entries ($2-$500) and a 60/25/15 prize split so ~9 of 12 entrants lost money; breeding fees funded prize pools; immortal horses with no burn caused supply inflation. It peaked mid-2021 (8-10k daily active owners, $20m a16z round, horses selling for $100k+), stalled from Sept 2021, and was shut down 28 Feb 2025, relaunching as auto-matched "Zed Champions" on Base with no NFT carry-over. Photo Finish LIVE (same studio as Horse Racing Manager) runs 24/7 races plus a monthly Virtual Kentucky Derby with official Churchill Downs partnership, but its audience is only "thousands"/hundreds of daily wallets.

### Cited Findings

**Zed Run: racing, economy, breeding (snapshot Dec 2021)**
- Launched early 2019; sold 4,450 horses in 2019 (~$150k total); live 3D races in 2020; growth surged Q2 2021 after the move to Polygon; raised $20m led by TCG and Andreessen Horowitz in July 2021 — [Naavik deconstruction, 16 Dec 2021](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/316789/ethereum-nft-game-zed-run-reborn-base).
- ~43,000 wallets owned a horse; ~9,275 stables raced in the week analysed; estimated 8-10k daily active owners (<1% of Axie Infinity's DAU); >50% of traffic from the US; top 500 holders (1.2%) controlled 24% of horses, the largest owning 727 — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/).
- Races: 12 horses, running constantly ("often several per minute"); free and paid races, entry $2-$500; prize split 60/25/15 for 1st-3rd (max 7.2x return); only top 3 profit, ~9 of 12 entrants lose money per paid race; ZED's rake cut to 0 — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/).
- Stamina: performance drops below 90% stamina; full recovery takes 12 hours. Horses were later restricted to their own class (could previously race up). Class-point rule changes in Nov and Dec 2021 (e.g., placing 7th or worse loses 2 points) — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/).
- Free races: ZED funded up to $20,000/day across up to 888 races (~$22/race). Tournaments: an October 2021 tournament had 15 qualifying days and a $300k prize pool; sponsored Netflix race had $3,000 buy-in and $75,000 pool — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/).
- Breeding: males up to 3 covers/month, females 1/month; horses never age or die, no burn mechanism. Public stud fee split: stallion owner 40-56%, ZED 15%, rest to prize pool (an earlier split was 60% stud owner / 15% prize pool / 10% retirement treasury / 15% ZED) — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/); [Zed Run Medium: Minimum Breeding Price](https://medium.com/@zed_run/minimum-breeding-price-and-allocation-4946bbd1634e) (via search summary).
- Genotype "Z-number": Z1 best, Z268 worst; offspring Z = sum of parents' Z. Bloodlines Nakamoto > Szabo > Finney > Buterin; breed types Genesis > Legendary > Exclusive > Elite > Cross > Pacer. ZED set minimum stud fees (80% bloodline, 20% breed type). Z10-or-better stallions sired 78% of non-genesis horses — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/).
- Spend and payouts: at least $14.8m spent on stud fees; ZED claimed $44.8m won by players across 694,000 races; >$120m of NFTs traded on OpenSea in 2021; a horse sold for $158k (May 2021); a Z1 skin sold for ~$55k — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/). Other reporting: one horse sold for $125,000, rare horses regularly >$15,000 in 2021 — [Sportico 2021](https://www.sportico.com/business/tech/2021/zed-run-nft-horse-racing-1234627668/) (via search summary).
- Criticisms (Dec 2021): wallet/WETH/OpenSea onboarding and gas fees; hidden genes/stats and closed-server race resolution; an insider-trading incident (team member bought 4 horses on internal info; ZED admitted and compensated); price controls (minimum stud fees, breeding limits) distort the market and narrow the meta; races are straight with no weather/terrain and "little gameplay"; class bands created exploitable "U-shaped" performance curves and frequent rule changes; asset values track ETH; horse inflation; stagnating users since Sept 2021 and poor communication — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/).
- Naavik's suggestions then: free-to-play non-cash product, demo/tutorial, more gene/breeding transparency, new race types that add depth without invalidating horses, and horse-care activities beyond racing and breeding — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/).

**Zed Run: decline and shutdown**
- 2022: still millions of dollars in monthly secondary volume (CryptoSlam) — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/316789/ethereum-nft-game-zed-run-reborn-base).
- 6 Feb 2025: Virtually Human announced Zed Run would close 28 Feb 2025 after six years, amid struggles with its community and falling asset prices; a 3-day "Sunset Stakes" (26-28 Feb) carried a $100,000 ZED token prize pool; week-long seasons with XP multipliers; reward badges carried over, horse NFTs did not; some holders called it a "scam"/"rug" on X — [Blockchain Gamer](https://www.blockchaingamer.biz/amp/news/37092/zed-run-discontinued-for-zed-champions/).
- NFT trading volume fell from ~$28,000 (Feb) to $627 (Mar) per CryptoSlam; legacy horses listed from ~$0.20 on OpenSea — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/316789/ethereum-nft-game-zed-run-reborn-base).
- Zed Champions (Base): horses race automatically, matched by skill (no manual entry); ZED token used for entry, breeding, augments; each of 4,320 horses holds an internal ZED balance that rises/falls with performance; retire to cash out; forced retirement if balance too low — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/316789/ethereum-nft-game-zed-run-reborn-base).
- Secondary claim: horse values fell >90% in many cases; breeding added supply faster than demand — [data40 analysis](https://data40.com/articles/zed-run-as-an-investment-case-growth-economics-and-key-mistakes/) (low-authority source).

**Photo Finish LIVE (Third Time Entertainment)**
- Founded by Ian Cummings (ex-Creative Director, EA Madden NFL); official gaming partner of the Kentucky Derby since 2023; "24/7 virtual horse races"; a monthly Virtual Kentucky Derby (14th running by July 2024) on a replica Churchill Downs; free starter horse on sign-up; "thousands" of stable owners; one owner chased the VKD for about a year (two 2nds before a win) — [KentuckyDerby.com, Jul 2024](https://www.kentuckyderby.com/horses/news/photo-finish-live-unlocks-a-new-generation-of-horse-racing-fans/).
- Month-long seasons lead up to each Virtual Kentucky Derby; runs on Solana with $DERBY and $CROWN tokens (track ownership via staked $CROWN) — [DappRadar blog](https://dappradar.com/blog/photo-finish-live-horse-racing-game-solana) (via search summary; page returned 403 on fetch).
- DappRadar (Sept 2025): "hundreds" of active wallets daily — [DappRadar](https://dappradar.com/dapp/photo-finish-live) (via search summary).
- Company claims it is the first real-money horse racing game approved by Apple — [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/projects/photo-finish) (company claim, unverified). Third Time describes it as real-money gaming with "the world's most advanced" breeding algorithm (marketing) — [Third Time Games](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=4376).

### Inferences
- Zed Run's failure modes are directly relevant to Post Time's economy rules: (1) paid entry with top-3 payouts means most players lose most races, which only works while asset prices rise; (2) immortal horses + no sink = supply inflation; (3) designer price floors and opaque genetics narrowed the meta; (4) racing itself had little decision depth, so the economy was the game. Post Time's choices (soft currency not cashed out, no prize money, retirement/death/aging, transparent Mendelian genetics, varied conditions) address each of these.
- Zed's very high cadence (several races/minute) diluted each race's spectacle; Post Time's single Live race every 3 minutes plus 10 entries/day creates scarcity and a shared moment instead.
- Photo Finish's monthly marquee race (VKD) with month-long seasons and a real-world licence is the closest analogue to a Season Final; a year-long chase for one title shows the pull of a prestigious recurring final even with a small audience.
- Zed Champions' move to auto-matched racing suggests manual entry chores did not retain; however auto-play also removes agency. Post Time's entry budget makes each manual entry a decision rather than a chore.

### Gaps
- No reliable peak or end-of-life player counts for Zed Run beyond the Dec 2021 Naavik estimate; no layoff reporting found.
- Photo Finish LIVE race cadence, entry fees, VKD qualification rules and current DAU not confirmed from primary sources.
- Zed Champions post-launch traction (2025-2026) not found.

## Q4. Other comparables: Horse Racing Manager, Rival Stars Horse Racing, Star Stable Online, Derby Owners Club, Howrse, Horse Reality

### Takeaway
The long-lived horse games retain via (a) a steady content cadence (Star Stable: weekly updates since 2011), (b) social structure (clubs, families), (c) a breeding collection that compounds over months/years (Howrse 2005-2026, Horse Reality), and (d) for racing-centric titles, frequent live PvP slots (Horse Racing Manager: live PvP races every 5 minutes) plus layered derbies/cups/championships. Derby Owners Club pioneered the "horse retires to stud after 20 races" persistent-card loop.

### Cited Findings

**Horse Racing Manager (Third Time, mobile, 2018-present)**
- Released 2 May 2018 (Tilting Point publisher at launch per search summary); still updated (v9.1.20, 11 Feb 2026); live PvP races "every five minutes" on "HRM TV, the perfect hub for online horse racing"; local derbies, circuits and cups with a global leaderboard and an "Ultimate Championship"; training via food, rest and surface-specific work; breeding compatible-trait horses passes on attributes, some skills obtainable only through breeding; "Breeding Rights" events unlock unique colours or "super speed"; horse trading; F2P with IAP; ~36.8k ratings averaging 4/5 — [Skich listing](https://skich.app/games/horse-racing-manager-2018).

**Rival Stars Horse Racing (PikPok)**
- Mobile, PC, then PS5 and Switch; Steam ~93% positive of ~4,168 reviews, tagged Online PvP; reviewers praise graphics and breeding but call racing repetitive/simplistic and gold/items for breeding hard to obtain; F2P with paid items — [SteamSpy](https://www.steamspy.com/app/1166860); [PocketGamer breeding guide](https://www.pocketgamer.com/rival-stars-horse-racing/breeding-guide); [The Mane Quest review, 2020](https://themanequest.com/blog/2020/6/21/review-rival-stars-horse-racing-desktop-edition-gorgeous-graphics-and-forgivable-flaws); [ThumbCulture PS5 review](https://www.thumbculture.co.uk/rival-stars-horse-racing-ps5-review) (via search summary).

**Star Stable Online (Star Stable Entertainment, 2011-present)**
- Grew from free Starshine Legacy games (2005) built to retain a horse book club; MMO launched 2011; committed to weekly updates since 2011 (new horses, gear, quests, races); core narrative >300 hours; clubs (guilds); 2020: DAU +39% to >150,000, MAU +26% to >630,000, revenue doubled to $44m; audience tween/teen girls and families (mothers/daughters, lapsed players returning with their daughters); churn as players age into young-adult interests; CEO calls it "incredibly sticky" — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/rise-star-stable-online/).
- Monetization: "Star Rider" membership (1 month, 3 months, lifetime "Pay Once") unlocks the full map, quests and horses past level 5; members receive 100 Star Coins every Saturday — [Star Stable Help Center](https://help.starstable.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001373620-What-s-the-Difference-Between-a-Star-Rider-and-a-Non-Star-Rider); [Star Rider wiki](https://starstable.wiki.gg/wiki/Star_Riders). Price range cited from $7.49/month to $69.95 lifetime — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/game-reviews/star-stable) (third-party, may be dated).

**Derby Owners Club (Sega arcade, 1999/2000)**
- Developed by Sega AM3/Hitmaker; NAOMI board, Lindbergh-board sequel 2008; each horse lives on a card storing training, feeding and race data, scannable on any cabinet; after 20 races a horse can be retired to the breeding stable as sire/dam, after which it can never race again; World Edition reached the US 2002; NTRA licensing deal — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derby_Owners_Club); [Sega Retro](https://segaretro.org/Derby_Owners_Club). Release year conflicts (1999 vs 2000) across sources.

**Howrse (Owlient/Ubisoft, browser 2005-present)**
- Launched 8 Sep 2005 (French version Equideow same day); Ubisoft acquired Owlient July 2011; 24 languages by 2015; >12m players in 2010 with ~2m new players/month; 55m claimed in 2014; Ubisoft now cites >60m (cumulative); an early microtransaction pioneer (passes -> equus currency); ~90% female players outside Iberia/Brazil, German players ~55% aged 10-15 (2009); criticism from German consumer advice centre (2009) and a 2011 TV report of a child buying passes 267 times — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howrse); [Ubisoft](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/company/about-us/our-brands/howrse) (via search summary).
- Still maintained: iOS app v5.0.14 updated 18 Mar 2026; third-party estimate ~5.6k iOS MAU (mobile only) — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/684830457) (via search summary).

**Horse Reality (Studio Deloryan, browser/Steam Early Access)**
- Genetics "based on real life science and genetic mapping" with ">half a billion" combinations; the team deliberately omitted some real findings (e.g., a second grey allele) because of art/system cost — [premortem.games, Dec 2024](https://premortem.games/2024/12/20/horse-reality-by-studio-deloryan-strikes-the-right-balance-between-science-and-gameplay/).
- A player blog (Aug 2025): horses age 1 year per 16 real days; speeding it requires mostly paid currency; argues bred horses become competitively "irrelevant" within months; praises realistic coat breeding — [Aywren](https://aywren.com/2025/08/26/horse-reality-browser-based-horse-breeding-simulator).

### Inferences
- Horse Racing Manager's "live PvP race every 5 minutes on HRM TV" is the nearest live-cadence precedent for Post Time's 3-minute Live race, and it has lasted 8 years with derbies/cups layered on top.
- Long-lived horse games (Star Stable 15 years, Howrse 21 years) skew young and female and depend on social/collection loops more than competition; Post Time's audience may differ, but clubs and visible collections are cheap retention.
- Derby Owners Club's fixed 20-race career before stud creates a natural turnover sink and a decision point (race on vs retire); Post Time's lifecycle (stud vs retire) is in this lineage.
- Horse Reality shows the flip side of real-time aging: power creep from breeding makes older horses obsolete quickly, which can feel punishing unless leagues/brackets segment by age or rating.

## Q5. Cross-game patterns: competition frequency, qualification, daily time, layered daily/weekly/monthly goals

### Takeaway
Across the genre, the common rhythm is: a short daily session gated by stamina/entries (Umamusume Team Trials 5-bar RP refilling every 2 h; Zed Run 12 h stamina recovery), a weekly ladder with promotion lines, and a monthly marquee event with multi-day qualifying and a single-race final (Champions Meeting; Photo Finish's monthly Virtual Kentucky Derby; Dabimas annual championship with 3 qualifier races). Fail-and-retry is built in through capped multiple attempts across days and consolation brackets.

### Cited Findings
- Daily: Umamusume Team Trials (1 RP per match, 5 RP max, +1 per 2 h) and Daily/Legend Races (1 Star Piece/day on a win) — [Deltia's: Team Trials](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=281576); [Deltia's: Legend Race](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=279218). Daily time estimates range from ~10 minutes (missions only) to 30-60 minutes, with "2-3 hours" for full dailies — [Deltia's: Daily Missions](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=279816) (via search summary).
- Weekly: Team Trials class promotion with a rising promotion line, retain rewards at Class 6 — [Deltia's: Class Levels](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=286982). Zed Run's final months used week-long seasons with XP multipliers — [Blockchain Gamer](https://www.blockchaingamer.biz/amp/news/37092/zed-run-discontinued-for-zed-champions/).
- Monthly: Champions Meeting (~1 week, 3-day signup, 2+2 qualifying days, 4 attempts/day x 5 races, 1-day prep, 1-race final, A/B finals) — [umamusu.wiki](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Champions_Meeting); [dbltap](https://www.dbltap.com/guides/umamusume-champions-meeting-pvp-mode-explained-schedule). Photo Finish monthly Virtual Kentucky Derby after month-long seasons — [KentuckyDerby.com](https://www.kentuckyderby.com/horses/news/photo-finish-live-unlocks-a-new-generation-of-horse-racing-fans/).
- Longer: Zed Run tournament with 15 qualifying days and $300k pool (Oct 2021) — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/); Dabimas national championship: qualifier = total points over 3 races, final months later (Jan entries -> 20 Mar 2026 final) — [gamebiz](https://gamebiz.jp/news/418813).
- Live cadence: Horse Racing Manager live PvP every 5 minutes — [Skich](https://skich.app/games/horse-racing-manager-2018); Zed Run "often several per minute" — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/); Photo Finish "24/7" — [KentuckyDerby.com](https://www.kentuckyderby.com/horses/news/photo-finish-live-unlocks-a-new-generation-of-horse-racing-fans/).
- Segmentation: Umamusume Graded vs Open League (Open cap B -> A+ from 31 May 2026) — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/umamusume-champions-meetings-changes-coming-in-may/); Zed Run class restriction (horses locked to own class) — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/).
- Retention evidence: Umamusume overseas mobile revenue halved from M2 ($22.5m) to M3 ($10.4m); content/banner updates produce multi-week spikes; media tie-ins did not — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/umamusume-pretty-derby-races-to-100m-on-mobile-in-one-year-overseas/). Star Stable's weekly updates and 300-hour narrative underpin 630k MAU (2020) — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/rise-star-stable-online/).

### Inferences
- Post Time's planned cadence (Live race every 3 min, 10 entries/day, weekly League promotion/relegation, weekday Feature Qualifiers, Saturday Weekend Feature, 4-week Season Final) is denser than Umamusume's (monthly CM) but sits between Horse Racing Manager (5-min live) and Photo Finish (monthly marquee). The 10-entry daily budget is roughly comparable to Umamusume's ~12 RP/day Team Trials allowance.
- Umamusume's multi-attempt qualifiers (4 attempts/day over 2 days per round) are a strong fail-and-retry model: Post Time's weekday qualifiers could similarly allow several tries per day with best-result counting, and a consolation "B Final" to keep mid-tier stables in the Season Final.
- Publishing conditions (track, distance, going) for the Weekend Feature and Season Final in advance, as Champions Meeting does, creates the "prepare a horse for the cup" loop that drives breeding/training decisions days ahead.
- A rising weekly promotion line (Team Trials) plus "retain" rewards at the top tier avoids a dead top tier; relegation should be soft for new players (separate rank-capped league as in Open League).

### Gaps
- No primary telemetry on average session length or D1/D7/D30 retention for any of these games was found.
- No data on how qualification difficulty (percentage reaching finals) is tuned in Champions Meeting.
